# Ether's Field

Beat-synced visuals for your own music, by Ether.

- **Modes:** Breath, Fascia, Ripple, Cymatic (sand, fire, water, air, Ether), Symbols, Platonic Solids, and Images (your elemental art and videos moving with the beat)
- **Sound:** add your own songs or videos, or play chakra tones (396–963 Hz)
- **Colour:** Ember, Tide, Moss, Dusk and the seven chakra palettes

Open it at **https://tonyparker1964.github.io/ethers-field/** and use *Add to Home Screen* to install it like an app. It works offline after the first visit. Everything you add stays on your device.
